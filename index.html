Our Legacy

Crafted by Hands, Cherished by Generations

Since 1998, Matri Jewellers has been the name families trust for their most precious moments — from weddings to festivals. Every ornament that leaves our atelier is a promise of purity, artistry and heritage.

We blend the grandeur of royal Indian craftsmanship with modern design sensibility, so every piece feels timeless yet of its time.
